# Fonts for Elegance

Fonts that convey classiness, sophistication, luxury, and refinement. The
perception of elegance in typography comes from specific structural
characteristics, not just cultural association.

## What makes a font look elegant

Three structural properties consistently signal elegance across studies:

1. **High stroke contrast** — large difference between thick and thin strokes.
   Transitional and Didone serifs (Bodoni, Didot, Playfair Display) exemplify
   this. The contrast creates visual tension and refinement (source:
   designmodo-font-psychology.md).

2. **Thin strokes and light weights** — light, airy fonts manifest feelings of
   subtlety and elegance. Luxury items typically feature thin, elegant,
   uppercase serif fonts whose spaced-out characters suggest attention to detail
   and premium quality (source: designmodo-font-psychology.md).

3. **Calligraphic influence** — letterforms that reference pen-drawn origins
   without sacrificing legibility. Script fonts score high on the Evaluative
   factor (perceived beauty and value) in Shaikh & Chaparro's research (source:
   designmodo-font-psychology.md).

## Elegant script fonts

Script fonts score highest on the Evaluative factor but lowest on Potency — they
read as beautiful but not strong (source: designmodo-font-psychology.md).

| Font | Character | Source |
|------|-----------|--------|
| Bickham Script | Thin-stroke luxury, formal calligraphy | designmodo-font-psychology.md |
| Edwardian Script | Sophistication, formality | designmodo-font-psychology.md |

Script fonts should be limited to display use (logos, headings, accents). They
fail at body text sizes due to legibility constraints (source:
todaymade-font-psychology-guide.md, brandvision-typography-influence-perception.md).

## Brand precedents

- **Cadillac** employs script fonts reflecting elegance and luxury (source:
  designmodo-font-psychology.md)
- **Chanel** uses script to convey charm and an expensive vibe (source:
  brandvision-typography-influence-perception.md)
- **Netflix** uses serif fonts for historical programming to signal gravitas
  (source: designmodo-font-psychology.md)

## Elegance through spacing

Elegance is not only about the typeface. Generous letter-spacing (tracking) on
uppercase text and wider line-height both contribute to a refined, unhurried
feeling. Luxury brands consistently use spaced-out characters (source:
designmodo-font-psychology.md). See [[whitespace]] and [[typography]].
