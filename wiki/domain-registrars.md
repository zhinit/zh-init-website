# Domain Registrars

A domain registrar is an ICANN-accredited company that sells domain registrations to the public. There are over 2,000 accredited registrars worldwide. The registrar you choose affects price, renewal costs, DNS management quality, and transfer friction. (source: dynadot-registering-first-domain.md, instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

## What to evaluate

- **Registration vs. renewal price** — the #1 cost factor. See [[domain-registration]] for the renewal price trap.
- **Total cost of ownership** — add renewal, WHOIS privacy (should be free), DNSSEC, SSL, and any "protection" add-ons.
- **TLD coverage** — ranges from 300 to 805 across registrars. Confirm support before committing to a niche TLD.
- **Free WHOIS/RDAP privacy** — standard in 2026. If a registrar charges for it, count it as part of their real price.
- **Transfer policy** — good registrars make outbound transfers easy. Bad ones add friction (slow unlock, blocked auth codes, outbound fees).
- **DNS management** — you spend more time editing DNS than searching for the domain. Clean panels matter.
- **Checkout upsells** — ranges from none (Cloudflare, Porkbun) to aggressive (GoDaddy).
- **API access** — relevant for developers or portfolio managers.
- **Security** — registry lock and mandatory 2FA for high-value domains.

(source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

## Individual profiles

### Cloudflare Registrar

At-cost pricing with zero markup. A `.com` is $10.46 total: $10.26 wholesale (VeriSign) + $0.20 ICANN fee. Same price for registration and renewal forever. Includes free WHOIS redaction, DNSSEC, and SSL via Cloudflare's edge. Tight integration with Cloudflare DNS, CDN, Workers, and DDoS protection. Requires a Cloudflare account. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md, cloudflare-registrar-docs.md)

Limitations: ~390 TLDs (narrower than others), most ccTLDs unsupported, no bulk search, no domain search exploration UI. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Porkbun

Flat pricing at $11.08 for `.com` (registration = renewal). Every domain includes free WHOIS privacy, free SSL (Let's Encrypt with auto-renewal), free URL and email forwarding. No upsells at checkout. Clean, modern interface. ~400 TLDs. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: basic search, bulk search capped at 100, email-only support. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Spaceship

Launched by Namecheap in 2023 as a ground-up rebuild. Near-wholesale pricing (~$9.98 for `.com`). Cleanest checkout interface in the industry. Shares registry access with Namecheap. 5+ million domains under management. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: younger product, smaller TLD catalog, immature API. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### NameSilo

6+ million domains. Flat pricing with a Discount Program: `.com` drops to ~$11.05 with a $50 account balance, as low as $7.75 for large portfolios. Free WHOIS privacy forever. No upsells. Built-in marketplace. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: plain interface, ticket-based support. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Dynadot

Widest TLD selection (~805). Built-in aftermarket marketplace. Mature API with 50+ commands. Bulk search up to 5,000/day on Super Bulk accounts. IDN (internationalized domain name) support. `.com` at $10.88 flat. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: best tiers require significant annual spend, utilitarian interface. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Namecheap

~17 million domains, 560+ TLDs. Free WhoisGuard. Beast Mode bulk search (up to 5,000 domains). Aggressive intro pricing with strong promo cycles. 24/7 live chat. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: renewal markup (`.com` goes from $10.98 → $18.48 in year 2), upsells at checkout. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Hostinger

Bundles domain with hosting. Free domain on most annual hosting plans (first year). AI website builder integration. 300+ TLDs. 24/7 live chat. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: low prices require multi-year prepayment, renewal jumps ($1.99 → $13.99). (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### Squarespace Domains

Absorbed Google Domains in 2023. 360+ TLDs. Clean DNS management inherited from Google Domains. Free WHOIS and SSL. Tight integration with Squarespace builder and Google Workspace. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: higher pricing (~$20+ for `.com`), no bulk search, no API. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

### GoDaddy

Largest retail registrar worldwide. 545+ TLDs. Bundles domains with hosting, email, website builder, SSL, and marketing tools. 24/7 phone support. Aftermarket via Afternic. Free standard WHOIS privacy on new registrations (recent change). (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

Limitations: aggressive upsells at checkout, `.com` renewals ~$21.99, "Full Domain Privacy" upsell at $9.99/yr beyond the now-free basic version. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)

## Search privacy

When you search on a registrar's site, that registrar logs every domain you check. This data has commercial value. Some registrars have been accused of domain front-running (searched-but-not-purchased domains getting registered by others shortly after). WHOIS/RDAP privacy protects your ownership record from the public — not from the registrar. (source: instant-domain-search-best-registrars-2026.md)
