# Hacker News

Overview of Hacker News (HN) submission rules, moderation mechanics, and
self-promotion policies. Run by Y Combinator; primary moderator is Dan Gackle
(dang). Contact: hn@ycombinator.com.

## Content Standard

On-topic: "anything that good hackers would find interesting," prioritizing
intellectual curiosity over entertainment. Off-topic: politics, crime, and
celebrity news unless they reveal novel trends (source:
hn-official-guidelines.md).

Blog posts, technical write-ups, research, and essays are submitted as regular
link submissions — not Show HN (source: syften-hn-posting-guide.md).

## Self-Promotion Rules

The core rule: "Don't use Hacker News primarily for promotion." Posting your own
work occasionally is fine if your normal use of the site is curiosity and
discussion (source: hn-official-guidelines.md).

What triggers moderation:

- Account that **only** posts links to its own blog/site (source:
  syften-hn-posting-guide.md)
- Soliciting upvotes or comments from anyone (source:
  hn-official-guidelines.md)
- Deleting and reposting failed submissions (source:
  syften-hn-posting-guide.md)
- Resubmitting recent duplicates that had significant engagement (source:
  syften-hn-posting-guide.md)
- Disguising landing pages as Show HN (source: syften-hn-posting-guide.md)

Safe approach: participate in discussions on other posts regularly. When you do
post your own content, it should be a fraction of your overall HN activity.

## Title Rules

- No uppercase emphasis
- No exclamation points
- No praise language ("the best", "amazing")
- No site names in the title
- No gratuitous numbers
- No editorialized framing
- Submit the original source, not a repost

Moderators will rewrite clickbait titles (source: hn-official-guidelines.md,
syften-hn-posting-guide.md).

## Post Types

| Type | Use for |
|------|---------|
| Regular link | Blog posts, research, essays, technical write-ups, news |
| Show HN | Interactive projects you made that people can try/run/inspect. Not for blog posts, newsletters, landing pages, or sign-up pages |
| Ask HN | Genuine questions seeking community input |

(source: syften-hn-posting-guide.md)

## What Reaches the Front Page

Research by Amplify Partners found blog posts are the largest front-page
category. Corporate announcements rarely appear. Content succeeds when it offers
something to analyze, test, debate, or learn from (source:
syften-hn-posting-guide.md).

Strongest angles:

- Technical lessons learned during development
- Surprising failures or tradeoffs
- Firsthand narratives with quantified outcomes
- Testable tools, demos, or datasets
- Well-reasoned arguments inviting informed disagreement

(source: syften-hn-posting-guide.md)

## Timing

No magic hour. Post during US business hours on weekdays when you can actively
respond to comments — typically 9 a.m.–12 p.m. Eastern. Be available after
posting; don't post before meetings or sleep (source:
syften-hn-posting-guide.md).
